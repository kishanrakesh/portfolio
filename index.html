About Me

I'm Kishan Rakesh, a Computer Science PhD student at The University of Texas at Dallas, where I research flaky tests and automated test repair with Professor Shanto Rahman. I work on why tests fail without the code changing, and on fixing them automatically.

Before the PhD I spent two years as a software engineer at Maybank, building the backend for a loan-origination system deployed across 3,000+ bank branches. I completed my M.S. at UT Dallas in Professor Shiyi Wei's lab, where my registered report on web accessibility received in-principle acceptance at ESEM 2026. I hold a B.Tech in Information Technology from NIT Karnataka.

I like problems where the failure is hard to reproduce and the fix has to be proven rather than argued — which is most of what testing research is. I'm looking for software engineering internships where that kind of rigour is useful, and I'm always happy to talk about testing, tooling, or research.

Experience

The University of Texas at Dallas

Graduate Research Assistant — Flaky Tests & Automated Test Repair

Research on test reliability with Professor Shanto Rahman. Fixed a flaky test in Apache TinkerPop, tracing it to unordered ConcurrentHashMap iteration: 10/10 NonDex seeds failed before the fix and 20/20 passed after. Dated the defect to its introducing commit and showed the test had been flaky for its entire 4.5-year history, which made git bisect inapplicable as no passing ancestor existed.

Graduate Researcher — Automated Repair of Web Accessibility Labels

Research in Professor Shiyi Wei's lab. Formulated accessible-name repair as link prediction over the accessibility tree, where the MLP beat the strongest heuristic by 0.34 mean Hit@10.
