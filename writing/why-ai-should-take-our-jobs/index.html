Why AI Should Take Our Jobs

Kishan Rakesh · October 2026

People think tech workers are f*cked because AI will take all our jobs. I agree that AI will take our jobs, but I think that's awesome.

Right now is the best ever time to be in tech. It's a golden age: if you can use coding agents, you can build pretty much anything in the world. You just need to want to.

To explain why tech workers are not actually f*cked and why tech layoffs don't have to be bad (for you), I present

THE KISHAN THEORY OF ECONOMICS

Please make it famous.

How we see ourselves

Here's how most of us view ourselves. The organization has a lot of money. The box is the job. Below us are our skills and tools. The organization gives us a little bit of its money, and we use our skills and tools to do the job.

The problem in tech

Now here's the problem. The AI takes one less dollar than you do, and saves the organization that extra dollar.

From the organization's perspective, this makes perfect sense. They're trying to be efficient. A smart company will do this. A company that doesn't realize it can save money replacing you is a dumb company. If you're employed at a dumb company, lucky you (for now).

Musical chairs

So what are people doing about it? Searching for that rare tech job at a dumb company. But the reality is, most companies are smart, so you're going to struggle to find one.

To me this is a really, really, really bad game to play. Think about it. If an AI is doing your role at one company for cheaper than you, then any company that hires you is wasting money. If an AI has your skills, your skills are bullsh*t. Even if you find a job, you're just an inefficiency that hasn't been found out yet.

Zoom out

So you might be thinking, “You're making it sound very f*cked for tech workers.” This sounds like a reasonable conclusion, but it rests on a myopic view of what jobs even are: something an organization gives you.

You have to zoom out.

The company only has all that money because there's demand, and it grows because demand keeps growing. That's where all the f*cking money is. Capitalism, baby.

A job isn't a gift from the holy tech companies. You get one only because you help them fulfill more demand.

So just go to the demand.

“But one person can't compete with a company”

This is true. One person can't outcompete a company alone. But you don't have to. Notice the size of the demand.

Big demand. Big company. Big bucks.

Smaller demand. Smaller you. Smaller bucks.

And the smaller bucks are still about what that job was paying you.

You're a link too

“But I don't know anything. I don't have the skills or knowledge to build some crazy sh*t.” Guess what? You don't need to know sh*t. The organization didn't know sh*t either. That's why it hired you.

You're not the bottom of the chain. You're just one of the links, like the organization.

You can look below you, the way the organization does, and make the same move they made. If they saved money by replacing you with a bot, it means a bot is cheaper than you. Way cheaper. And the good news there: it also means you can afford a few.

That's why AI taking our jobs is awesome. The AI that replaced you can work for you, and you can afford a whole team of them.

Don't see yourself as a calf suckling at the udder of the organization, waiting to be replaced by a less hungry calf.

You can be the organization now.
